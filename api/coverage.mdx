## History

- **Lighter** trade history starts on **30 August 2025**. Wallets that traded before then can have incomplete
  early history, so their all-time numbers start from that date.
- The **equity curve** (`/positions/equity`) starts no earlier than **1 January 2026**; its realized line still
  carries the full lifetime total from before that date.
- The **Copy Score** only uses the last 90 days.

## Perpetuals only

Analytics cover perpetual futures. Spot trades on venues that also list spot markets are excluded.
