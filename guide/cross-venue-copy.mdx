---
title: "Cross-venue copy"
description: "Follow a trader on one venue, trade on another."
---

You do not have to trade on the same venue as the trader you copy. Pick an account on another venue in **Copy
with**, and Perp.ag places the copy there.

| Trader on | Can be copied onto |
| --- | --- |
| Lighter | Lighter, RH Lighter, Phoenix |
| RH Lighter | Lighter, RH Lighter, Phoenix |
| Phoenix | Lighter, RH Lighter, Phoenix |
| Jupiter | Lighter, RH Lighter, Phoenix |

Bullet copies are same-venue only.

## How it differs from a same-venue copy

- **Only markets listed on both venues are copied.** A trade on a market your venue does not list is skipped.
- **Orders are market orders.** Limit orders and limit-order mirroring are not available.
- **Leverage is always a maximum.** Your copy never goes above the leverage you set.

<Tip>
  Cross-venue copy lets you copy a strong Jupiter trader on Lighter, for example. Prices differ slightly
  between venues, so compare a [paper copy](/guide/paper-copy) on your venue before going live.
</Tip>
