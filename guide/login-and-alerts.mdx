## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Login in the app">
    The Perp.ag Telegram bot opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the bot">
    It sends you back to the app with a one-time code, and you are signed in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

There is no wallet-connect login: your Telegram account is your Perp.ag account. If someone shared a referral
link with you, open it before you log in so the referral is applied.

## Security codes

Withdrawals and key exports ask for a **6-digit code** that the bot sends to your Telegram. Nobody can move
your funds or export a key without access to your Telegram account, so keep it secure (turn on Telegram's
two-step verification).

## Alerts

- **Notify on trade** (per copy): an alert for every trade that copy places. See
  [Copy trading](/guide/copy-trading).
- **System Notifications** (**Settings** in the account menu): browser or app push notifications for trades and
  position updates.
