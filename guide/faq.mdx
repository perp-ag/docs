---
title: "FAQ"
description: "Common questions about Perp.ag."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Perp.ag hold my funds?">
    No. Copies trade in your own accounts on each venue, and you can export each account's key from
    [Accounts](/guide/accounts) at any time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is my result different from the trader's?">
    A copy fills shortly after the trader, at the price available then, and pays its own fees. Size caps,
    leverage limits, markets and sides you excluded, and trades skipped for slippage also change the outcome.
    **Trade Logs** shows every copied order, and a [paper copy](/guide/paper-copy) shows the leader trades it did
    not copy and why.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to my positions when I stop a copy?">
    Nothing. Pausing or unfollowing stops new copied trades; positions the copy already opened stay in your
    account for you to manage.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I copy more than one trader?">
    Yes, with one trader per account. Create a subaccount for each trader you want to copy, so each copy has its
    own balance and its own [Fund protection](/guide/fund-protection).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I trade by hand on an account that copies?">
    You can, but the copy and your own trades share the same positions on a market, and **Force-flatten** fund
    protection closes everything. Use a separate account for manual trading.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How far back does trader history go?">
    Lighter history starts on 30 August 2025, so wallets older than that can have incomplete early history.
    The Copy Score only uses the last 90 days.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Perp.ag open to everyone?">
    Not yet. Perp.ag is in private testing while we harden copy execution against live markets. Follow
    [@perp_ag on X](https://x.com/perp_ag) for the public launch.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use Perp.ag data in my own product?">
    Yes. See the [Data API](/api/overview).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
