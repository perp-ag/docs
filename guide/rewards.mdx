title: "Rewards and referrals"
description: "Earn POINTS from your trading and from the traders you bring in."
---

Open **Rewards** in the top bar to see your POINTS, your level, your referral code and your referral network.

## Earn from referrals

<Steps>
  <Step title="Share your code">
    Copy your referral code or link from the **Rewards** page and share it with friends and traders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They sign up through your link">
    Anyone who opens your link before logging in is linked to you as a Level 1 referral. People they refer
    become your Level 2 referrals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earn POINTS on their volume">
    You earn **30%** of your Level 1 referrals' trading volume and **10%** of your Level 2 referrals' trading
    volume as POINTS.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Levels

POINTS = trading volume × your level multiplier. Higher trading volume unlocks a larger multiplier.

| Level | Multiplier |
| --- | --- |
| Level 1 | 1.0x (base) |
| Level 2 | 1.1x (+10% bonus) |
| Level 3 | 1.2x (+20% bonus) |
| Level 4 | 1.3x (+30% bonus) |
| Level 5 | 1.4x (+40% bonus) |

The **Rewards** page shows how much more volume you need to reach the next level, your total POINTS, and your
Level 1 and Level 2 referrals.
