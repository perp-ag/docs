---
title: "Backtest"
description: "Replay a trader's past positions against your own settings."
---

A backtest answers: *if I had copied this wallet with these settings, what would have happened to my
account?* It replays the wallet's past positions at your size and charges fees and slippage on every trade.

## Run a backtest

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the trader's profile">
    Find the wallet in [Explore](/guide/explore) and open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Backtest">
    The backtest uses the profile's selected range (7D to ALL).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your inputs">
    | Input | Meaning |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Starting capital ($)** | The balance you would start with. |
    | **Copy %** | The share of each of the trader's positions you would take. |
    | **Max per-trade ($)** | A cap on any single copied position. |
    | **Markets** and **Sides** | Copy only some markets, or only longs or shorts. |
    | **Taker fee %** and **Slippage %** (Advanced) | The cost charged on every simulated fill. |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the result">
    **Final Value**, **ROI**, **Max Drawdown**, **Win Rate**, **Trades Copied**, **Total Fees** and an equity
    curve of your simulated account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A backtest assumes you got the trader's prices plus the slippage you set. Real copies fill a little after
  the trader, so live results trail a backtest. Use [paper copy](/guide/paper-copy) to measure that gap on live
  markets before you commit money.
</Note>
