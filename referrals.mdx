# How Referrals Work

At Perp.ag, we believe in rewarding our users for spreading the word about our platform. Our referral program is designed to be simple and lucrative.

## Two Levels of Referrals

Our referral program has two levels, which means you can earn from both your direct referrals and the people they refer.

- **Level 1:** You earn 30% of the trading volume from the users you directly refer to Perp.ag.
- **Level 2:** You earn 10% of the trading volume from the users that your Level 1 referrals bring to the platform.

This two-tiered system provides a powerful incentive to not only bring new users to our platform but also to encourage them to refer others.

## How to Get Started

1.  **Get Your Referral Link:** You can find your unique referral link in your Perp.ag account dashboard.
2.  **Share Your Link:** Share your link with friends, family, and on your social media channels.
3.  **Start Earning:** You'll automatically start earning a percentage of the trading volume from your referrals.
