# Getting Started

This guide will walk you through the first steps to get your Perp.ag account set up: logging in and depositing funds.

## How to Login

We use a secure and simple login method through Telegram.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Login With Telegram">
    On the Perp.ag app, click the **"Login With Telegram"** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Your Telegram">
    Our bot will send a unique, secure login link to your Telegram account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Login Link">
    Open the message in Telegram and click the link to be automatically and securely logged into your Perp.ag account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Deposit Funds

To start copy trading, you'll need to deposit funds into your Perp.ag wallet.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the Deposit Button">
    Once logged in, navigate to your wallet and click the **"Deposit"** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send Funds">
    A unique wallet address will be displayed. Send the funds (USDC or SOL) you wish to trade with to this address from your personal wallet (e.g., Phantom, Solflare).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirmation">
    Your balance will be updated as soon as the transaction is confirmed on the Solana blockchain. You are now ready to start copy trading!
  </Step>
</Steps>
